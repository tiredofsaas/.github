# tired of saas

We got tired of paying a monthly fee for everything.

Software we already own gets a feature pulled behind a higher tier. A tool we used for years turns into a subscription. A simple thing we need every day is sold back to us at a price that makes no sense for the size of the problem.

So we started building the small things ourselves. This is where we put them.

Everything here came out of real work. Someone needed it, built it, used it, and cleaned it up enough to share. None of it is polished product. All of it works, because we use it.

## What you'll find

Small tools that do one job. Connectors that let AI assistants work with the software people actually have installed. Scripts that replace a paid add-on with a few hundred lines of code.

Right now: [revu-mcp](https://github.com/tiredofsaas/revu-mcp), an MCP server for working with PDF drawings and markups when Bluebeam Revu is on the machine. More as they come out of the shop.

## How it's licensed

Free for personal and non-commercial use under the PolyForm Noncommercial License. If you want to use something here inside a business, open an issue and ask. We're not against it, we just want to know.

Nothing here cracks, patches or works around a vendor's licence. When a feature isn't licensed, our tools do the job another way or tell you they can't. No vendor software, documentation or files are redistributed. Product names are only used to say what a tool works with. We're not affiliated with any of those companies and they haven't endorsed any of this.

## We could use help

This is a few people doing this around their day jobs. If you've been burned by the same thing and can write code, test on a machine we don't have, or just write a clear bug report, you're welcome here.

- Pick an open issue and comment on it.
- Pull requests are reviewed. Keep them small and explain what problem they solve.
- Got a tool of your own that fits? Open an issue and tell us about it.

No roadmap, no deadlines, no support contract. Just people fixing the things that annoy them, in the open, for anyone who has the same problem.
